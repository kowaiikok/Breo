Terms & Conditions

Last updated: 18 May 2026. These terms explain how member orders, products, payments, vouchers, delivery, branch fulfilment, and customer support work on this website.

Product Conditions

  • Product names, images, prices, and product codes shown on this website are for customer ordering and branch fulfilment reference.
  • Massage guns, eye massagers, neck massagers, and portable massage products should be used according to the product guide and safety instructions.
  • Customers should stop using a product if it causes discomfort, pain, irritation, dizziness, or any unusual reaction.
  • This website does not provide medical advice. Customers with medical conditions should consult a qualified professional before using massage or recovery devices.

Delivery & Branch Fulfilment

  • Orders may be fulfilled from Bukit Jalil or 1U / 1 Utama depending on the selected branch, stock, pickup arrangement, and delivery address.
  • Estimated delivery dates shown in the customer dashboard are estimates only. Actual timing may change based on stock, courier availability, public holidays, weather, address accuracy, and branch processing.
  • Customers must provide a complete delivery address and reachable phone number. The branch may contact the customer if the address is incomplete or unclear.
  • Branch staff may update the order status to packing, ready for courier, shipped, or completed after admin acceptance.

Payment Terms

  • The checkout flow supports credit or debit card, e-wallet, and Atome-style instalment options as a website payment concept.
  • Orders are sent to admin review after the customer completes the payment step and provides a payment reference.
  • Admin may decline an order if payment, product details, customer contact details, or delivery information is missing, suspicious, duplicated, or invalid.
  • Refund, cancellation, and exchange rules should follow the official Breo business policy once the website is used commercially.

Members, Verification & Vouchers

  • Customers must create a member account and verify their email before placing an order.
  • New member and birthday vouchers may be shown by popup or in the vouchers section. Voucher availability may depend on eligibility, birthday date, campaign timing, and branch approval.
  • Sales associate codes may assign the order to a retail associate such as Louis, Kowaii, Win, Rongyi, or SY for follow-up.
  • Vouchers cannot be exchanged for cash and may be changed, paused, or removed by the business.

Privacy & Order Processing

  • The website may collect member name, email address, phone number, gender, birthday, delivery address, selected products, payment reference, sales associate code, and order status.
  • Order details may be visible to admin and branch staff so they can accept, decline, pack, ship, and support the order.
  • The AI helper on this website is a guided order assistant for summarising products, delivery, branch selection, and support next steps. Final order decisions are made by admin or branch staff.
  • Customers should not enter sensitive card numbers or banking passwords into the demo payment reference field.
